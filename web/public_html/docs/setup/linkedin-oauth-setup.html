LinkedIn OAuth Setup

LinkedIn OAuth enables professional account authentication using "Sign In with LinkedIn using OpenID Connect". SIGNula gets access to basic profile information and email address, providing a trusted authentication option for professional and business-oriented users.

Overview

LinkedIn's authentication system uses the OpenID Connect (OIDC) protocol, which is built on top of OAuth 2.0. When a user signs in with LinkedIn, SIGNula receives their basic profile information (name, headline, profile picture) and email address. This is particularly popular for business-oriented applications and professional user bases.

OpenID Connect Standard

LinkedIn has migrated to the OpenID Connect standard, replacing the older OAuth 2.0 scopes (r_liteprofile and r_emailaddress). SIGNula uses the modern openid, profile, and email scopes for authentication.

Prerequisites

  • A LinkedIn account
  • Access to the LinkedIn Developer Portal at linkedin.com/developers
  • A LinkedIn Company Page (required to create an app)
  • A publicly accessible domain with HTTPS enabled for your SIGNula instance
  • A Privacy Policy URL for your application
  • Access to the SIGNula Admin Panel
Important: LinkedIn requires that you associate your app with a LinkedIn Company Page. If you do not have one, you will need to create a Company Page on LinkedIn before proceeding.

Step-by-Step Setup

1

Go to LinkedIn Developers

Visit linkedin.com/developers and sign in with your LinkedIn account. You will be taken to the LinkedIn Developer Portal.

2

Create a New App

Click the Create App button in the top navigation.

3

Fill in App Details

Complete the form with the following information:

  • App name: SIGNula Authentication
  • LinkedIn Page: Select your company page or create a new one
  • Privacy policy URL:
https://yourdomain.com/legal/privacy
  • App logo: Upload your organisation's logo (100x100 px recommended)
4

Create the App

Click Create app. You will be taken to the app management dashboard.

Note: You may be asked to verify your association with the LinkedIn Company Page. A page admin will need to approve the verification request.
5

Navigate to the Auth Tab

In the app dashboard, click on the Auth tab. This is where you will find your credentials and configure OAuth settings.

6

Add Redirect URL

Under OAuth 2.0 settings, click the pencil icon next to Authorized redirect URLs for your app and add: 

https://yourdomain.com/oauth/callback

Click Update to save the redirect URL.

Important: The redirect URL must match exactly, including the protocol (https://) and path. No trailing slash.
7

Copy Client ID and Client Secret

On the Auth tab, locate and copy the following credentials:

  • Client ID: Displayed directly on the page
  • Client Secret: Click Show to reveal, then copy
Critical: Keep your Client Secret confidential. Never expose it in client-side code, public repositories, or browser-accessible files.
8

Navigate to the Products Tab

Click on the Products tab in the app dashboard.

9

Request "Sign In with LinkedIn using OpenID Connect"

Find Sign In with LinkedIn using OpenID Connect in the products list and click Request access.

Good news: This product is usually auto-approved and access is granted immediately. You should see it move to the "Added products" section within moments.

Once approved, the openid, profile, and email scopes will be available for your app.

10

Configure SIGNula Admin Panel

Log in to your SIGNula Admin Panel and navigate to:

System → OAuth Providers → LinkedIn

  1. Toggle the Enabled switch to activate LinkedIn OAuth.
  2. Paste your Client ID into the "Client ID" field.
  3. Paste your Client Secret into the "Client Secret" field.
  4. Click Save.
Security: The Client Secret is stored encrypted in tblSettings (marked as isSensitive) and is never exposed in the admin UI after saving.
11

Test the Login

Open your SIGNula login page and click the Sign in with LinkedIn button. You should be redirected to LinkedIn's consent screen, and upon approval, returned to SIGNula with your account linked.

Success: If login completes without errors and your LinkedIn profile information appears in SIGNula, the integration is working correctly.

Scopes & Permissions

SIGNula requests the following OpenID Connect scopes from LinkedIn:

Scope Description Required
openid OpenID Connect authentication. Enables the use of the ID token for user identity verification. Yes
profile Access to basic profile information including name, headline, and profile picture URL. Yes
email Access to the user's primary email address associated with their LinkedIn account. Yes
Deprecation Notice: LinkedIn has deprecated the older scopes r_liteprofile and r_emailaddress in favour of the OpenID Connect standard scopes listed above. If you are migrating from an older integration, update your scopes accordingly.

Important Notes

Company Page Requirement

LinkedIn requires that every developer app is associated with a LinkedIn Company Page. If your organisation does not have one, create it at linkedin.com/company/setup/new before creating the app.

"Sign In with LinkedIn" Product

The "Sign In with LinkedIn using OpenID Connect" product must be added to your app from the Products tab. Without it, authentication requests will fail with a scope error. This product is typically auto-approved.

Token Lifetime

LinkedIn access tokens have a default lifetime of 60 days. With the offline_access scope (if supported), refresh tokens can be used to obtain new access tokens without requiring the user to re-authenticate. SIGNula handles token refresh automatically.

API Rate Limits

LinkedIn API rate limits apply to all requests. The specific limits depend on your app's usage tier. For authentication-only usage (as with SIGNula), you are unlikely to hit rate limits under normal circumstances. Monitor the X-Li-RateLimit-Remaining response header if needed.

Troubleshooting

  • Ensure you have added the Sign In with LinkedIn using OpenID Connect product from the Products tab.
  • Verify the product shows as "Added" in your app dashboard.
  • If you recently added the product, wait a few minutes for it to propagate.
  • Check that you are using the correct scopes: openid profile email (not the deprecated r_liteprofile).

  • The redirect URL configured in the LinkedIn app must match exactly what SIGNula sends in the OAuth request.
  • Check for protocol mismatches (http vs https).
  • Ensure there are no trailing slashes or extra path segments.
  • Verify the redirect URL in the SIGNula settings matches the one in LinkedIn: oauth.linkedin.redirect_uri.

  • LinkedIn requires all apps to be associated with a Company Page.
  • Create a Company Page at linkedin.com/company/setup/new.
  • You must be an admin of the Company Page to associate it with your app.
  • If you select an existing page, the page admin may need to approve the verification request.

  • App verification requires a page admin to confirm the association between the app and the Company Page.
  • Check the Company Page admin's LinkedIn notifications for a pending verification request.
  • The app can still be used in development mode while verification is pending.
  • If rejected, ensure the correct Company Page was selected and retry the verification.

  • Ensure the profile and email scopes are included in the OAuth request.
  • Some LinkedIn users may have restricted their profile visibility, which can limit the data returned.
  • Verify that the "Sign In with LinkedIn using OpenID Connect" product is active on your app.
  • Check the SIGNula activity log for the raw API response from LinkedIn to identify missing fields.

Settings Reference

These settings are stored in tblSettings in the SIGNula backend database. Fields marked with are encrypted at rest.

Setting Key Type Description
oauth.linkedin.enabled Boolean Enable or disable LinkedIn OAuth as a login provider
oauth.linkedin.client_id String Your LinkedIn app Client ID
oauth.linkedin.client_secret String Your LinkedIn app Client Secret (encrypted, isSensitive)
oauth.linkedin.redirect_uri String OAuth callback URL (e.g., https://yourdomain.com/oauth/callback)
oauth.linkedin.scopes String Space-separated list of OAuth scopes. Default: openid profile email
